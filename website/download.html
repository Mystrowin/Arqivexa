Current supported release

Download Arqivexa 0.5.0 Stable for Windows 11 x64.

Arqivexa 0.5.0 Stable is the sole supported release. It creates, opens, edits, saves, extracts, and optimizes .cfs archives through an Explorer-first workflow.

Keep independent backups of important archives.

Arqivexa is compression software, not a backup system. Versions 0.1.0 through 0.4.0 are withdrawn and unsupported.
